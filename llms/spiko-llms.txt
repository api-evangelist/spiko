# Spiko

> Spiko is a European fintech (MiFID-authorized investment firm, ACPR license #19183) offering regulated daily treasury management: tokenized, UCITS-approved government money market funds and multi-currency cash accounts (EUR, USD, GBP, CHF). Businesses and individuals earn risk-free-rate yield on cash while keeping daily liquidity. Client funds are custodied by CACEIS Bank; Spiko holds no client funds. Spiko exposes three developer APIs — a public fund-data API, an Investor API, and a Distributor API.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from apis.yml and the captured artifacts in this repository.

## APIs

- [Public API](https://docs.spiko.io/developers/public_api/getting_started): Open, unauthenticated access to fund data — funds, share classes, NAV history, yields, exchange rates, fund assets. Base URL https://public-api.spiko.io/v0
- [Investor API](https://docs.spiko.io/developers/investor_api/getting_started): Direct clients/partners manage accounts, deposit/withdrawal/transfer orders, portfolio, and yields. Base URL https://investor-api.spiko.io/v1 (HTTP Basic or OAuth 2.0)
- [Distributor API](https://docs.spiko.io/developers/distributor_api/getting_started): Distributors manage investors, accounts, orders, contracts, documents, wallets, and webhooks on behalf of their clients. Base URL https://distributor-api.spiko.io (HTTP Basic)
